## What this is

Adservice Influencer was the landing page for Adservice's influencer network, designed in 2022 while I was lead designer at Adservice. The Figma file has three frames: desktop at 1440px, iPad at 768px and iPhone at 320px.

We never finished it. The project was paused when Adtraction bought Adservice, so what follows is the design as it stood, including the placeholder copy and figures still in the file.

Adtraction bought Adservice in early 2024, and today I design [Iriz](../iriz/), Adtraction's app for influencers. So I have now worked on the same problem twice, at two companies that became one: getting a brand and a creator to the same place when they want opposite things from it.

## Two visitors, one page

A brand wants reach it can measure. An influencer wants a sponsor that fits their feed and pays. The page asks which one you are in the hero and splits there.

| Where | Brand | Influencer |
|---|---|---|
| Hero promise | "Connect with your target audience in a non-intrusive way through influencers" | "Advertise brands your audience love and earn money in the process" |
| Hero button | I'm a brand | I'm an influencer |
| Contact pitch | "Reach customers in a non-intrusive way by using our network of influencers." | "Keep your work's integrity intact by finding a matching brand to sponsor you." |
| Third form field | Brand | Profile link |
| Submit | Get in Contact | Apply as Influencer |

Both forms ask for a name and an email. The third field is where they part: a brand gives its name, an influencer gives a link to their profile.

## Down to 320px

![The contact section stacked on a phone](/projects/adservice-influencer/mobile-contact.jpg)

On iPad the contact forms stay side by side. On a 320px phone the hero turns yellow and its buttons stack. The category grid becomes a full-width tile, two half-width tiles and another full-width tile. The two contact forms stack with a rule between them, brand first.

## Then and now

![Left: the two phones from the 2022 Adservice Influencer page. Right: the Discover feed in Iriz, with brand stories, an editorial card and per-click rates.](/projects/adservice-influencer/then-and-now.jpg)

| | Adservice Influencer (2022) | Iriz (2024) |
|---|---|---|
| Medium | Responsive website | iOS and Android app |
| Who it talks to | Brands and influencers, split on one page | Influencers only |
| Getting in | Apply through a form | Invite code, then connect a social channel |
| What the influencer sees first | A pitch and a form | Brands and their per-click rate |
| Proof of earnings | A reporting dashboard shown on a laptop | Earnings on every link card |

The 2022 page sells the network and ends in a form. Iriz puts the network inside the app, so an influencer sees brands, rates and earnings before anyone from sales is involved.
