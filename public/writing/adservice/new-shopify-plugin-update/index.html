ADSERVICE SHOPIFY APP

Today we are happy to announce the arrival of the new Adservice Shopify Tracking App

The Shopify App allows you to easily integrate tracking with your e-commerce website.
Integrate now!

WHAT DOES THE SHOPIFY APP DO?

It assists you in integrating Adservice Tracking with your Shopify webshop.

  • Whenever consumers delivered through the Adservice Affiliate Network buys something from your webshop, Adservice's system will be notified and track the sale on your behalf.

  • Live tracking conversions will greatly improve the amount and quality of clicks your webshop will receive.

  • The sales amount of the basket will be sent to Adservice, so we can calculate the conversion price.
