# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: hiring managers and design leads scouting Jonatan for a senior product design / design engineering role. They arrive from a job posting, a referral, or a recruiter link, usually on desktop or phone, with only a few minutes to decide whether his work is credible and whether he thinks like a senior.

Secondary: peers, recruiters, freelance clients, and friends who land via shared links. They skim for proof of craft and a way to reach him.

## Product Purpose

A single-site portfolio that gets Jonatan hired. It presents his case studies, shipped products, and design engineering craft well enough that a design lead wants to talk to him. Success means booked interviews and inbound opportunities, not vanity traffic.

## Positioning

AI-aware design engineering: Jonatan designs systems and builds what he designs, with disciplined AI-assisted implementation. He ships real products end to end (YADL, a Figma design-linter plugin used by 500+ users globally, and Yet Another Countdown, a widget app), rather than showing only mockups.

## Operating Context

Next.js 16 + React 19 + Tailwind 4 static-export site (TypeScript, MDX case studies under content/projects/). Deployed as static output. Written in English; Jonatan is based in Denmark and open to relevant roles. Contact via email and LinkedIn.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Case-study-driven structure: each project gets a written case study (MDX), not just screenshots.
- Known case studies include YADL, LeadPlatform, Adtraction brands work, Billigskadedyr redesign, Iriz, and Yet Another Countdown.
- Site is a static export; no backend, no CMS. Content changes happen in MDX files.
- Open decision: which case studies to lead with and how to order them is still being tuned.

## Brand Commitments

- Name and personal brand: Jonatan Bjerre-Kær, UX designer and UX engineer.
- DESIGN.md is binding (may evolve deliberately): the design language derives from YADL (canonical baseline Yet-Another-Design-Linter, origin/main at dd51730), Open Runde typography, Animate UI for interaction-feedback icons, Lucide for static icons, restrained YADL palette.
- Signature thesis line, used sparingly where it fits: "AI almost never says no. Design is the discipline of saying no."
- Voice: precise, plain, human-signed writing. No AI-slop, no hype vocabulary, no em/en dashes.

## Evidence on Hand

- DESIGN.md (root): binding design language, approved 2026-07-15.
- content/projects/*.mdx: real case studies with project copy and media.
- Real adoption numbers may be cited: YADL 500+ users globally (Figma weekly email, 2026-09-01), 60+ frequent daily users.
- linkedin.pdf in repo root.
- Do not fabricate: testimonials, client names, awards, traffic numbers, or employer claims not present in the case-study content.

## Product Principles

1. Proof over polish: every claim about the work must trace to a real shipped artifact or case study.
2. Judgment is the feature: show decisions, constraints, and trade-offs, not only final screens.
3. Design-to-code credibility: the site itself demonstrates the craft it advertises.
4. Respect the reader's minutes: a hiring manager must get the signal in the first screen.
5. Human-signed voice: copy reads like a person wrote it, specific and restrained.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Follow WCAG 2.1 AA: visible focus states, sufficient contrast on the light palette, reduced-motion respected (see DESIGN.md motion rules).
